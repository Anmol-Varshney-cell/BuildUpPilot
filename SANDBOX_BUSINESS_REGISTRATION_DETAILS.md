# Sandbox Business Registration Details Guide

## 📋 **Required Business Information for Sandbox**

### **1. Business Name**
- **What to enter**: Your official business/company name
- **Examples**: 
  - "BUILD UP PILOT Technologies"
  - "Career Platform Solutions"
  - "Digital Education Services"
- **Format**: Legal business name as registered
- **No**: Personal names or abbreviations

### **2. Business Type**
- **Options**: 
  - **Private Limited** (Pvt. Ltd.)
  - **Limited Liability Partnership** (LLP)
  - **Partnership Firm**
  - **Proprietorship** (Sole Proprietor)
  - **Public Limited** (Ltd.)
  - **One Person Company** (OPC)
- **Choose**: Your actual business registration type
- **Example**: "Private Limited"

### **3. Business Website**
- **What to enter**: Your official business website
- **Format**: Full URL with https://
- **Examples**:
  - "https://builduppilot.com"
  - "https://careers.builduppilot.com"
  - "https://education.builduppilot.com"
- **If no website**: Enter "https://example.com" (temporary)

### **4. Contact Email**
- **What to enter**: Official business email address
- **Format**: professional email
- **Examples**:
  - "contact@builduppilot.com"
  - "support@builduppilot.com"
  - "admin@builduppilot.com"
- **Avoid**: Personal email addresses

### **5. Contact Phone Number**
- **What to enter**: Official business phone number
- **Format**: With country code
- **Examples**:
  - "+91-9876543210" (India)
  - "+1-555-123-4567" (US)
  - "+44-20-1234-5678" (UK)
- **Mobile**: Business landline or mobile number

### **6. Business Address**
- **What to enter**: Complete registered business address
- **Format**: Full address with pincode
- **Example**:
  ```
  123, Business Complex,
  Andheri East,
  Mumbai, Maharashtra - 400069
  India
  ```

## 📄 **Business Registration Documents**

### **Required Documents**
1. **Business Registration Certificate**
   - **Format**: PDF/JPG
   - **Content**: Official registration document
   - **Details**: Business name, address, registration number

2. **PAN Card**
   - **Format**: PDF/JPG
   - **Content**: Company PAN card
   - **Details**: 10-digit PAN number

3. **Address Proof**
   - **Format**: PDF/JPG
   - **Content**: Recent utility bill or rent agreement
   - **Details**: Business address matching registration

4. **Bank Account Details**
   - **Format**: PDF/JPG
   - **Content**: Cancelled cheque or bank statement
   - **Details**: Business bank account for verification

## 📝 **Tips for Successful Registration**

### **Business Name**
- ✅ Use exact legal name as per registration
- ✅ Include "Pvt. Ltd." or "LLP" if applicable
- ❌ Don't use abbreviations or short forms

### **Website**
- ✅ Use your actual business website
- ✅ Ensure website is accessible
- ✅ Use HTTPS if available

### **Contact Information**
- ✅ Use professional email address
- ✅ Use business phone number (not personal)
- ✅ Ensure email and phone are working

### **Documents**
- ✅ Upload clear, readable PDFs
- ✅ Ensure documents are recent (within 6 months)
- ✅ Use color scans if available
- ✅ File size under 5MB per document

---

## ⚠️ **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

### ❌ **Don't Do:**
- Use personal name instead of business name
- Enter fake or temporary business details
- Upload unclear or expired documents
- Use personal email/phone numbers
- Provide incomplete address information

### ✅ **Do Instead:**
- Double-check all information before submission
- Use official business documents only
- Ensure all contact information is current
- Keep digital copies of all documents
- Be prepared for verification calls

## 🎉 **After Registration**

### **What Happens Next:**
1. **Document Review**: Sandbox team reviews your documents (1-2 days)
2. **Business Verification**: May require additional information
3. **Account Activation**: You'll receive confirmation email
4. **API Access**: Get your API credentials immediately
5. **Start Testing**: Begin API integration with BUILD UP PILOT

### **Timeline Expectation:**
- **Registration**: 10-15 minutes
- **Document Review**: 1-2 business days
- **API Access**: Instant after approval
- **Total Setup Time**: 1-3 days
