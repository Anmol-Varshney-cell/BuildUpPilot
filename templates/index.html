Privacy & trust

The Build Up Pilot Privacy Policy outlines a commitment to protecting student data while delivering a personalized placement experience. The platform collects essential information—including identity details, academic records, uploaded resumes, and audio recordings from AI mock interviews—to provide features like smart resume analysis, curated job discovery, and instant performance feedback. In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, the policy ensures that data is processed only with user consent for specific purposes, such as tracking learning progress and improving interview readiness. Students retain full control over their data, with the right to edit profiles, withdraw consent, or delete interview sessions at any time through their dashboard, while a dedicated grievance system is in place to address any privacy or technical concerns.

We treat student data with care. Only the minimum required information is stored to deliver features like interview history, resume feedback, and job tracking.
